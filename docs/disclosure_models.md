# Disclosure Models

This project distinguishes three disclosure models:

1. Full circuit disclosure: the circuit is public and resource claims can be recomputed.
2. Withheld circuit plus proof: the circuit is private but a proof artifact substantiates selected claims.
3. Public circuit plus certificate: the circuit or circuit family is public and accompanied by a structured certificate, deterministic tests, and resource metadata.

`qecdlp-cert` targets the third model.
